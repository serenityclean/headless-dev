How it works

Three steps from idea to launch.

A clear spec, a fixed price for every phase, and working software you can review at each step.

  1. 01

    Written intake, spec and fixed quote

    You fill out a detailed written intake. We reply in writing within two business days with a plain-language spec covering features, screens, data and integrations, plus a fixed quote, or a straight answer that we are not the right fit. The spec and price go into a written agreement you approve before any build starts.

  2. 02

    Fixed-price build in phases

    The work is split into phases, each with a fixed price and a clear deliverable. You review working software and sign off before the next phase begins.

  3. 03

    Launch plus monthly support

    We deploy, move your data and get your team running. Monthly support covers fixes, updates and new features as the business grows.

You own everything. The code, the repositories, the hosting and every account are set up in your name. No lock-in.
